# ERA5 London Spike — 15 March 2024

## Scope

This is the first real-data validation of the Layer 2 weather pipeline. It uses
the CDS dataset `reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels`, not the newer
`reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels-timeseries` subset.

The request covers 15 March 2024, 00:00–23:00 UTC, a ±6° box around London,
five variables (`z`, `t`, `u`, `v`, and pressure velocity `w`), and the 13
pressure surfaces selected by `scripts/download_era5.py`. The first 12 hours
were converted to the runtime cube.

Local assets are gitignored:

- Raw NetCDF: `weather_data/raw/london-2024-03-15.nc`
- Runtime cube: `weather_data/cubes/london-2024-03-15T00.npz`
- Raw SHA-256: `ac42a5d20ab8a67b101bfb72ea075b7c6c134fc7245bbefbcd3bdb86008a0ae1`
- Cube SHA-256: `64f5723e1514ae5e506b1030b393efcffb1a930111bd533daa4cdfab85f89e9c`

## Integrity and physical checks

| Check | Result | Verdict |
|---|---:|---|
| Raw dimensions | 24 times × 13 pressure levels × 48 × 48 | Pass |
| Cube dimensions | 13 times × 81 altitudes × 48 × 48 | Pass |
| Time grid | 0–43,200 s, hourly | Pass |
| Altitude grid | 10–30 km, 250 m | Pass |
| Finite fields | All `u`, `v`, `w`, temperature and pressure values finite | Pass |
| Pressure monotonicity | 100% of adjacent altitude pairs decrease | Pass |
| Pressure round-trip at source surfaces | median 0.0066%, p95 0.064%, max 0.072% relative error | Pass |
| Temperature round-trip | median 0.061 K, p95 0.139 K, max 0.244 K absolute error | Pass |
| U-wind round-trip | median 0.058, p95 0.175, max 0.424 m/s | Pass |
| V-wind round-trip | median 0.042, p95 0.354, max 0.547 m/s | Pass |

The small round-trip error is expected from resampling source pressure surfaces
onto 250 m geometric levels and back. It demonstrates converter fidelity; it
is not an independent validation of ERA5 against observations.

Across the full 15–25 km operational band:

- Temperature: 199.44–219.08 K; median 206.81 K.
- Pressure: 2.208–11.945 kPa; median 5.157 kPa.
- Density: 0.0358–0.1951 kg/m³; median 0.0885 kg/m³.
- Horizontal wind: 1.16–31.02 m/s; median 17.02 m/s; p95 24.65 m/s.
- Only 0.024% of values exceed the 30 m/s observation normaliser.
- Converted geometric vertical wind: −0.0423 to +0.0555 m/s; median
  +0.00553 m/s. The sign convention is upward-positive via
  `w = -omega/(rho*g)`.
- Horizontal vector shear: median 2.41 and p95 5.42 m/s per vertical kilometre.

These ranges are physically coherent for the lower stratosphere and do not
show unit/sign failures.

## Vertical-resolution verdict

At the grid point nearest London, only four native pressure surfaces fall
inside 15–25 km:

| Pressure | Geopotential height |
|---:|---:|
| 100 hPa | 15,988 m |
| 70 hPa | 18,188 m |
| 50 hPa | 20,192 m |
| 30 hPa | 23,206 m |

Their vertical gaps are approximately 2.20, 2.00, and 3.01 km. Consequently,
the 250 m output is smooth interpolation rather than 250 m information. The
pressure-level product is suitable for exercising the pipeline and broad-scale
atmosphere, but **not accepted as the production training source for
altitude-selection policy learning**. The next data experiment should use
ERA5's 137 model levels and compare resolved shear before building the corpus.

## Size and runtime

- Raw file: 6.07 MB.
- Compressed runtime cube: 40.29 MB (39 MiB on disk).
- In-memory arrays: 46.28 MiB; measured process RSS increase 55.95 MiB.
- Cube load: 0.53 s.
- NetCDF-to-cube conversion: about 4 s on the development laptop.
- Passive runtime benchmark (42,376 steps before the domain-related numerical
  abort): 7,240 physics steps/s, or 2.01 simulated hours per wall-clock second,
  including normal environment stepping.

This is comfortably usable on the laptop and should not be a compute concern
on the HPC. Keeping one short cube cached per worker remains appropriate.

## Horizontal-domain finding

The ±6° request maps to approximately ±406 km east/west and −640/+666 km
north/south at London. In one deterministic passive run (seed 3):

- The trajectory first left the weather grid at 35,759 s (9.93 h).
- It spent 15.6% of its executed steps using spatially clipped edge weather.
- It reached the 500 km numerical x-bound and terminated at 42,376 s
  (11.77 h), 824 s before the scheduled end.

One trajectory is not a production sizing distribution, but it is enough to
reject this longitude extent for full benchmarks. Production extent must be
chosen from a multi-date displacement study, with clipping measured explicitly.

## Conclusion

The download, coordinate conversion, pressure/temperature atmosphere,
horizontal wind, vertical-wind conversion, runtime format, and simulation path
all pass the first real-data spike. Two acquisition choices do not pass for
production: pressure-level vertical resolution and the ±6° longitude extent.
